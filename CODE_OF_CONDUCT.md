# Code of Conduct

Corridor is an open-source community project.

## Our standard

Be respectful, factual, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be civil in issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Focus on the code, docs, and product behavior
- Give clear, actionable feedback
- Respect maintainers and contributors

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment
- Hate speech
- Personal attacks
- Deliberate misinformation
- Disclosure of private credentials or sensitive data

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.
