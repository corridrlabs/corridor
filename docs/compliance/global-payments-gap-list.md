# Global Payments Compliance Gap List by Region
## Scope
Covers key regulatory requirements for payments, remittance, AML/CFT, data protection, and consumer protection across major operating regions. Assumes Corridor operates cross-border payments, wallets, and P2P transfers.

---

## 1. Africa Region
### Key Markets: Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana
#### Core Regional Framework
- African Union Data Protection Guidelines
- Regional AML/CFT standards (ESAAMLG, WAMLG, SADC FRP)
- Cross-border remittance rules (World Bank GPSS)

##### Kenya (Detailed in `/docs/compliance/kenya-compliance-gap-list.md`)
❌ No CBK PSP license | ⚠️ Partial KYC | ❌ No ODPC registration

##### Nigeria
**Legal Requirements**
- CBN Payment Service Provider License (Payment and Settlement Systems Act 2007)
- NFIU AML/CFT compliance (Money Laundering Act 2022)
- Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) registration with NDPC
- Remittance: CBN International Money Transfer Operator (IMTO) license

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No CBN PSP/IMTO license, no NDPC registration, no NFIU reporting workflow

##### South Africa
**Legal Requirements**
- FSCA Payment Service Provider license (Financial Sector Regulation Act)
- FICA AML/CFT compliance (Financial Intelligence Centre Act)
- POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act) registration with Information Regulator
- Cross-border: SARB exchange control approval

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No FSCA license, no POPIA registration, no FICA screening/monitoring

##### Ghana
**Legal Requirements**
- Bank of Ghana PSP license (Payment Systems and Services Act 2019)
- Anti-Money Laundering Act 2020 compliance
- Data Protection Act 2012 registration with DPC

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No BoG license, no DPC registration, no AML program

## 2. Europe Region
### Key Markets: EU, UK, Switzerland
#### Core Regional Framework
- EU PSD2 (Payment Services Directive 2)
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- AMLD6 (Anti-Money Laundering Directive 6)
- PCI DSS v4.0.1 for card processing

##### European Union
**Legal Requirements**
- National competent authority PSP license (e.g., ACPR France, BaFin Germany)
- PSD2 Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) for payments
- GDPR compliance: DPO appointment, 72h breach notification, data subject rights
- AMLD6: Beneficial ownership register, transaction monitoring, SAR reporting to FIUs

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No EU PSP license, no GDPR compliance, no AMLD6 program, no SCA implementation

##### United Kingdom
**Legal Requirements**
- FCA Payment Institution license (Payment Services Regulations 2017)
- UK GDPR compliance (Data Protection Act 2018)
- FCA/PRA AML/CFT compliance, SAR reporting to NCA
- PSR (Payment Systems Regulator) oversight for payment systems

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No FCA license, no UK GDPR compliance, no PSR registration

##### Switzerland
**Legal Requirements**
- FINMA Payment Service Provider license (Financial Institutions Act)
- FADP (Federal Act on Data Protection) compliance
- AMLA (Anti-Money Laundering Act) compliance, SAR reporting to MROS

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No FINMA license, no FADP compliance, no AMLA program

## 3. North America Region
### Key Markets: United States, Canada
#### Core Regional Framework
- US BSA (Bank Secrecy Act), Canada PCMLTFA (Proceeds of Crime and Terrorist Financing Act)
- State-level payment licensing (US), Provincial licensing (Canada)
- State privacy laws (US: CCPA/CPRA, Canada: PIPEDA)

##### United States
**Legal Requirements**
- State Money Transmitter License (MTL) in each operating state (46+ states)
- Federal FinCEN MSB (Money Services Business) registration
- BSA/AML compliance: CTR (Currency Transaction Report), SAR filing, OFAC sanctions screening
- PCI DSS Level 1/2 compliance if processing >1M/20M transactions annually
- State data privacy: CCPA (California), CPA (Colorado), etc.

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No state MTLs, no FinCEN MSB registration, no BSA/AML program, no state privacy compliance

##### Canada
**Legal Requirements**
- FINTRAC MSB registration (all provinces)
- Provincial payment services licensing (e.g., Ontario, Quebec)
- PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act) compliance
- PCMLTFA AML/CFT: SAR reporting, sanctions screening

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No FINTRAC registration, no provincial licenses, no PIPEDA compliance

## 4. Asia-Pacific Region
### Key Markets: Singapore, Australia, India
#### Core Regional Framework
- Singapore MAS PS Act, Australia AUSTRAC, India RBI PSS Act
- Regional data protection laws (PDPA, Privacy Act, DPDP Act)
- Cross-border remittance rules (APG, FATF)

##### Singapore
**Legal Requirements**
- MAS Payment Service License (Payment Services Act 2019)
- PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act) compliance, DPO appointment
- MAS AML/CFT: Transaction monitoring, SAR reporting to Suspicious Transaction Reporting Office (STRO)

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No MAS license, no PDPA compliance, no MAS AML program

##### Australia
**Legal Requirements**
- AUSTRAC remittance licence (Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act)
- APRA Payment Institution license (if offering wallet services)
- Privacy Act 1988 compliance, Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme
- ePayments Code compliance for consumer protection

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No AUSTRAC license, no Privacy Act compliance, no APRA registration

##### India
**Legal Requirements**
- RBI Payment Aggregator/PSP license (Payment and Settlement Systems Act 2007)
- PMLA (Prevention of Money Laundering Act) compliance, SAR reporting to FIU-IND
- DPDP Act 2023 (Digital Personal Data Protection) compliance
- Cross-border: RBI approval for remittance

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No RBI license, no DPDP Act compliance, no PMLA program

## 5. Latin America Region
### Key Markets: Brazil, Mexico, Argentina
#### Core Regional Framework
- Brazil BCB, Mexico CNBV licensing
- LGPD (Brazil), LFPDPPP (Mexico) data protection
- Regional AML/CFT standards (GAFILAT)

##### Brazil
**Legal Requirements**
- BCB Payment Institution license (Lei Geral de PAGAMENTOS)
- LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados) compliance, ANPD registration
- BCB AML/CFT: Transaction monitoring, SAR reporting to COAF

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No BCB license, no LGPD compliance, no COAF reporting

##### Mexico
**Legal Requirements**
- CNBV Payment Service Provider license (Ley para Regular las Instituciones de Tecnología Financiera)
- LFPDPPP (Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares) compliance
- CNBV AML/CFT: SAR reporting to UIF

**Current Status**: ❌ Non-Compliant  
**Gaps**: No CNBV license, no LFPDPPP compliance, no UIF reporting

3. **Data Protection**: GDPR/POPIA/NDPA compliance + DPO appointments
4. **Key Markets**: License applications for top 3 target markets
5. **Regional Consumer Protection**: Dispute resolution + fee transparency per region
6. **PCI DSS**: Global card data boundary audit + SAQ completion
