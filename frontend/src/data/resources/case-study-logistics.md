# Case Study: Logistics Co.
**Type**: Case Study
**Duration**: 5 min read
**Industry**: Logistics

## Overview
"SwiftLogistics" automated driver payouts and expense management.

## Challenge
Managing daily expenses and payouts for 200 drivers was a manual, paper-heavy process prone to fraud and delays.

## Solution
Corridor enabled:
- Instant expense approvals via mobile app
- Automated daily payouts to driver wallets
- Real-time fleet expense tracking

## Results
- **2 hour** reduction in daily processing time
- **15% savings** on operational costs
- **Real-time visibility** into fleet spend
