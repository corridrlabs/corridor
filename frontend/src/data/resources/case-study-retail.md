# Case Study: Retail Chain
**Type**: Case Study
**Duration**: 4 min read
**Industry**: Retail

## Overview
How "FreshMart" scaled to 50 locations using Corridor's unified platform.

## Challenge
FreshMart struggled with fragmented systems for POS, inventory, and payroll, leading to reconciliation errors and delayed reporting.

## Solution
They implemented Corridor to:
- Centralize all transaction data
- Automate supplier payments
- Offer EWA to 500+ employees

## Results
- **30% reduction** in administrative time
- **Zero** reconciliation errors
- **95% employee satisfaction** with EWA
