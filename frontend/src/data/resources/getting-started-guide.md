# Getting Started Guide for Business Owners
**Type**: Guide
**Duration**: 5 min read
**For**: Business Owners

## Overview
Welcome to Corridor! This guide will help you set up your organization and start managing your business operations efficiently.

## Key Takeaways
- How to create and verify your organization
- Inviting team members and assigning roles
- Setting up your first payment method

## Implementation Steps

### 1. Organization Setup
Navigate to **Settings > Organization** to complete your profile. Add your company logo, industry, and size to get personalized recommendations.

### 2. Team Onboarding
Go to the **Team** app to invite your colleagues.
- **Admins**: Full access to all features
- **Managers**: Can approve requests and view reports
- **Members**: Standard access for daily tasks

### 3. Verify Your Account
Complete the KYC process by uploading your business registration documents. This unlocks higher transaction limits and advanced features.
